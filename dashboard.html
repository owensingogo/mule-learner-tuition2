⚠️ Administration Notice

Only the authorized CEO should access this control centre. Payment approvals, teacher hiring, teacher suspensions and learner access should be handled carefully.
🔐 CEO Security

The CEO account will have higher permissions than teacher and learner accounts.

Teachers will not be able to approve their own accounts, approve payments or manage other teachers.

Learners will only have access to their own learning account.
