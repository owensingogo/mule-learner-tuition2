🔐 CEO Payment Control

Only the CEO should approve or reject learner payments.

When a payment is approved, the learner's learning access will be activated according to the selected plan.

When a payment is rejected, the learner will remain without paid access and will be shown the rejection reason.

Important: Always confirm that the payment has actually been received before approving a learner.
