⚠️ Administration & Teaching Notice

The CEO has the highest level of access. The CEO may teach learners when necessary, especially when an approved teacher is unavailable, absent or unable to conduct a scheduled class.

Teacher hiring, payment approvals, suspensions and learner access remain under CEO control.
🔐 CEO Security

The CEO account has higher permissions than teacher and learner accounts.

Teachers cannot approve their own applications, approve payments or manage other teachers.

Learners only have access to their own learning account.

The CEO can act as a backup teacher without needing teacher approval.
